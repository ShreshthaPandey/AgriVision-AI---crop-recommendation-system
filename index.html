KisanMitra

Kya ugayein? Ab data batayega.

Bahut se kisan har season mein andaze se fasal chunte hain, aur kabhi kam yield, kabhi loss ho jata hai. KisanMitra aapke district ka purana yield data, mandi bhav aur mausam dekh kar batata hai ki kaunsi fasal kitna accha perform karegi, kitni mehnat lagegi aur kitna profit ho sakta hai.

Problem

Galat fasal ka chunav, mausam ka andaza nahi, aur kamai ka hisaab pehle se nahi pata hota.

Hamara solution

Sirf State, District aur Season daaliye. ML model (LightGBM) 12 saal ke district yield data se fasal ki performance predict karta hai.

Kisan ko fayda

Low / Medium / High performance, mehnat (effort), mausam ka fit aur estimated profit ya loss ek hi jagah.

Crop Recommendation

Mausam (Open-Meteo data)

Estimated values. Price, cost aur ideal mausam ranges approximate hain, actual farm par badal sakte hain. Zyada profit wali fasal (Banana, Ginger, Turmeric) mein investment aur risk bhi zyada hota hai.
